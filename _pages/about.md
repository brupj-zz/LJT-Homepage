## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Professor Junxian He in the HKUST NLP Group. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I earned my B.Eng.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
